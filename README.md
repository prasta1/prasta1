# Patrick Ruster

Ten years selling and supporting enterprise SaaS — Sama, Faraday, DataRobot, CarGurus, Edge Impulse — and the last two years teaching myself to build the software instead of waiting on it.

Most of what's here is that: native macOS and iOS apps, and the local-inference infrastructure I run them against.

## The setup

A Mac Studio, a MacBook Pro, and a NAS wired into one Tailscale mesh, serving GGUF models through llama-swap to everything I build. Photos, DNS, monitoring, and the boring parts of my job hunt are self-hosted and automated. Building this is how I actually learned what enterprise customers mean when they say "we want to run it on-prem" — and it's the reason I'm now looking for a role on the AI/ML infrastructure side.

## What I've shipped

**Apps**

- **[Modelo](https://github.com/prasta1/Modelo)** (6★) — Native macOS client for local and cloud LLMs. LM Studio over Tailscale, OpenRouter, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint. Streaming chat with token metrics, agentic tool use, model load/unload, menu-bar mini chat. SwiftUI + SwiftData + Swift Charts, zero third-party packages.
- **[helm](https://github.com/prasta1/helm)** — Personal CRM for Mac, iPad, and iPhone. Kanban pipelines, contacts, Google Calendar, AI follow-up assistant.
- **[swell](https://github.com/prasta1/swell)** — Menu-bar app for surf conditions and crowd counts.
- **[taco-scout](https://github.com/prasta1/taco-scout)** — Finds nearby taco spots. One job, done well.
- **[hideout](https://github.com/prasta1/hideout)** — Ranks Northeast terrain for hollows, dense canopy, and defensible hidey-holes. Science in service of antisocial tendencies.

**Infrastructure & tooling**

- **[bottomless-hole](https://github.com/prasta1/bottomless-hole)** — Local, undetectable DNS ad sinkhole for macOS. Pi-hole-style blocking for every app on the machine, not just the browser. Go daemon with a native Tauri dashboard.
- **[hermes-agent](https://github.com/prasta1/hermes-agent)** — Forked and actively patched. It's the agent runtime behind most of the automation in this profile.

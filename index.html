The work

A sample of what I have shipped in customer success and business development. Every number is real and every artifact exists.

01

Product-led growth to enterprise motion

Built the full lifecycle marketing engine that converts self-serve developer users into paid customers without a hard sell.

  • Authored three behaviorally triggered email sequences (account, training, deployment activation) targeting exact funnel states, cadence from day 0 to day 21.
  • Wrote a 3-email PLG to enterprise sequence that surfaces the production licensing boundary as a fact, not a threat, earning replies instead of archives.
  • Every email opens with a specific observation of the user's real work. No mass email framing, no pressure.
02

PQL scoring redesign, driven by data

Led the analysis that broke a flawed lead scoring model. The old system rewarded marketing engagement over product usage; I showed it was broken and proposed the fix.

  • Analysis of 476 PQL-tagged contacts found 90% trained a model but never deployed, and zero corporate-email contacts deployed.
  • Diagnosed four root-cause hypotheses for the deployment gap and proposed replacing opaque scoring with a simple “Holy Trinity” definition: data, train, deploy.
  • Worked across RevOps, Product, and Engineering as part of the PQL tiger team.
03

Product analytics and expansion scanning

Wrote Python tooling that scores every organization in the product for upsell and outreach readiness.

  • Streaming scan scripts classify orgs by “Holy Trinity” activity (data, train, deploy) and compute a 0-100 expansion score from user growth, activity growth, and breadth.
  • An enterprise scan surfaces which accounts are expansion-ready; a PQL scan surfaces which non-enterprise orgs behave like real leads.
  • A validator queries the analytics API directly to confirm an account's ingestion, training, and deployment activity before a sales conversation.
04

Lead management framework for the CRM

Designed the operating system for how a sales organization manages leads.

  • Defined 8 lead stages with entry and exit criteria, SLA-based speed-to-lead rules by source (1 hour for demo requests, 4 for product-qualified leads), and a 7-touch outreach cadence.
  • Wrote the process doc and the training guide, so the framework is followed, not just filed.
  • Built the companion CRM account taxonomy separating account type, segment, tier, and lifecycle into clean orthogonal dimensions.
05

Customer intelligence system

  • Hundreds of contacts mapped with role, influence level, and last activity for the largest accounts.

07

Sales enablement and objection handling

Built the playbooks the sales team reaches for.

08

Customer health monitoring and dashboards

Automated the at-risk detection that used to live in one person's head.

How I work

The operating principles behind the work above.

Retention is a revenue motion

Health scores, renewal risk, and touch cadence are not hygiene. They are the pipeline for the next deal. I run them with the same discipline as the sales forecast.

Data before opinions

Product analytics, CRM exports, and usage signals drive every decision. When the data said the PQL model was broken, I showed the receipts before proposing the fix.

Build the system, not the one-off

I ship the scripts, templates, and playbooks so the motion works whether or not I am in the room. The artifacts above outlive any single meeting.

Curiosity beats pitching

The outreach that works starts from a real observation of the other person's work. That principle shapes every email and every account plan I write.
